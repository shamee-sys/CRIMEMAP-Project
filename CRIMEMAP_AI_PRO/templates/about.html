About CRIMEMAP AI

Geospatial Crime Intelligence and Decision Support System for Puducherry.

HACKATHON PROTOTYPE
⬡
PROJECT PURPOSE

Make spatial patterns easier to explore.

CRIMEMAP AI is an educational prototype demonstrating how incident records can be organized, mapped, filtered and summarized to support careful review and discussion.

04 · DATACSV file · Fictional records

Methodology & Limitations

  • Data ingestion

    Demo incident rows are loaded from a local CSV file.

  • Spatial display

    Coordinates are synthetic examples used to demonstrate map interactions.

  • Area ranking

    Hotspots are simple counts grouped by area; no validated predictive AI model is implemented.

  • Decision support

    Charts summarize this sample only. They do not prove causation or actual risk.

!
Demo data disclosure

Every incident record in this prototype is fictional. No official Puducherry police statistics are included. Do not use this prototype for operational policing, targeting, or real-world safety decisions.
